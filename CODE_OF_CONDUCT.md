# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in BRUTE Runtime's issues, discussions, and (once
opened) pull requests to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported using GitHub's
private vulnerability/abuse reporting channels for this repository, or
by opening an issue requesting a private contact channel (without
including sensitive details in the public issue itself). Reports will
be reviewed and addressed as promptly as this project's current
(volunteer, pre-1.0) capacity allows.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and are expected
to take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues,
discussions, pull requests once open) and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.
